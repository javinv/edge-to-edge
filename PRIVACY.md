# Edge to Edge privacy policy

_Last updated: 24 October 2026_

Edge to Edge does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- The Safari extension changes the layout settings of the web pages you open so they fill the outer display. It does not read, record or send the content of those pages, your browsing history or anything you type.
- The extension and the app make no network requests and include no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
- Your settings (the on/off switch and the list of sites where you turned it off) are kept on your device by Safari and are never sent anywhere.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/javinv/edge-to-edge/issues.
